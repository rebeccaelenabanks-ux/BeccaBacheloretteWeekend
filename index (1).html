Good to Know

A few details worth having on hand before the weekend.

Getting There

Thank you everyone for committing to the trip — we know that many of you are taking off work or using PTO. As we have ladies coming from various places, our goal is to make transportation as easy as possible. We're planning to carpool, and there will be a sign-up sheet for anyone who'd like to offer to be a driver. The bride will be driving herself.

  • 4-wheel drive is recommended in the mountains.
  • Tire chains may be required if there's been a storm — we'll keep an eye on conditions.
  • Gas up before you leave; stations get sparse once you're in the mountains.
  • Cell service can be spotty around Bass Lake, so it's worth downloading offline maps beforehand.
  • If carpooling, please expect to split gas with the driver.

  • Parking — carpool when possible and stick to designated parking areas.
  • Before we leave — double-check for chargers, clothes, toiletries, and anything that may be hiding under the bed!

Weather & Travel FAQs

What to expect from the Sierra in November, and a few common questions.

Weather Watch 🍌🏔

November in Bass Lake means crisp mountain air, chilly mornings, and cozy cabin weather! Weather in the mountains can change quickly, so come prepared for cooler temperatures and the possibility of rain or even early-season snow.

Pack layers, a warm jacket, and shoes you don’t mind getting a little muddy. We’ll keep an eye on conditions as we get closer!

🏟️ Check the Bass Lake Weather Forecast

Typical conditions

Daytime highs usually sit in the upper 50s to low 60s°F, with mornings and nights dropping into the 30s. Skies are often clear, but early-season rain or snow is possible.

On the trail

The granite around Willow Creek can get slippery with rain, frost, or early snow — we'll adjust the hike if conditions call for it.

Driving into Yosemite

Tire chains are sometimes required after a storm. We'll check road conditions before committing to the Yosemite option on Saturday.

Daylight

Sunset is early — around 4:50–5:00 PM — so outdoor plans wrap up by late afternoon.

What should I pack for the weather?

Warm layers, a jacket that blocks wind, gloves, and shoes with good traction for the hike. See the packing list above for the full rundown.

Is there cell service at the cabin?

Service can be spotty around Bass Lake. Worth downloading offline maps and letting people know you may be slow to respond.

What happens if it rains or snows?

We'll adjust the hike or Saturday's plan as needed — any changes will go out in the group chat.

Do we need snow chains for the drive?

Possibly, especially for the Yosemite option after a storm. We'll check CalTrans road conditions before heading out.

Should I bring cash?

A little is handy for small shops, but most places around Bass Lake and Oakhurst take cards.
